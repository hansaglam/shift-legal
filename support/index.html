Support

Need help with SHIFT: Chain Puzzle? This page covers common issues and how to contact support.

Common questions

  • Progress is stored on your device. Reinstalling the app or clearing app data may remove local progress.
  • Rewarded ads require an internet connection and may not always be available.
  • If a rewarded ad is closed before a verified reward is granted, no Hint or Undo reward is added.
  • Sound, haptics and language can be adjusted from Settings inside the game.

Contact

For support, bug reports, privacy questions or other inquiries, email: support@rito.app

Please include your device model, iOS/Android version, app version and a short description of the issue when possible.
